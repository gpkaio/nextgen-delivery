About

13 years as a dispatcher. Now building the agentic one.

I'm a Barbadian founder with more than seventeen years in high-volume, life-critical operations — including over thirteen years as an Emergency Medical Dispatcher at The Queen Elizabeth Hospital, Barbados' main hospital. Every shift I route help to people in real time, under pressure, and often with no formal street address to work from — locating them by landmark and description, with zero critical errors.

That lived expertise in real-time dispatch, routing and coordination is the foundation of NextGen Delivery.

The Venture

NextGen Delivery

A consumer delivery app — food, grocery, pharmacy and parcel — powered by a multi-agent AI dispatch-and-routing engine. Customers order by text, voice or WhatsApp and describe where they are by landmark; specialized agents handle intake, landmark-to-location resolution, merchant ordering, driver dispatch and routing, and automatic recovery when things go wrong, with humans confirming the moments that matter.

The problem

No formal addresses, fragmented couriers, slow cross-island movement, and small merchants priced out of professional logistics.

The solution

An agentic dispatch engine — the exportable core asset — that works without formal addresses, drawn directly from 13 years of hospital dispatch.

The moat

Landmark address resolution: a proprietary, compounding data advantage that global apps structurally won't build.

The scale

Built for Barbados, then CARIFORUM, then every emerging market with informal addressing. If it works in the Caribbean, it scales globally.
